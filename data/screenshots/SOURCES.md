# Screenshot sources

The screenshots and `page-turns.gif` were taken from Leggio itself. Every
score in them is public domain sheet music from the
[Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/), engraved by Mutopia's
volunteers with LilyPond from 19th-century editions. Each piece is marked
"Public Domain" on its Mutopia page and in the footer of its PDF. No scan
of a modern publisher's edition is used.

Titles and composers were filled in through Leggio's own "Edit Details";
the tags, setlist and bookmarks were made up for the screenshots.
